# Split

The shared tab for your flat, office or trip, on the BUSY Bar. Open it and see **everyone at once**: a card per person, green for what they get back, red for what they owe. Turn the wheel for **who pays whom**, boiled down to a short list of payments that settle everything.

## Log an expense

Press START, spin the wheel to the euros, then to the cents, pick who paid and who shares it, and press START at each step: **"Anna paid €12.40 for everyone"** is in, with a badge and a bell. Split suggests as payer whoever owes the most, so the tab evens itself out. Spinning fast jumps by tens, so big shops are quick too. Toggle people in and out, and Split shows each person's part as you go.

## Settle up

Each payment has its own page: Anna's avatar, **€35.00**, Ben's avatar, the money flowing between them. When Anna pays Ben, press START on it: who, to whom and how much are filled in, START twice and it is gone. Paid back only part of it? Turn the wheel first. The wheel also flips through everyone's balance: who gets money back and who owes.

## Mistakes happen

The menu keeps a history of the last 40 entries. Delete any of them, undo the last one, or clear the tab to start over. The back display shows the whole tab and the keys at all times.

## Set up

Put the names of 2 to 30 people in the app's settings, and pick your currency: euro, dollar, pound, franc or krona. Everything is kept on the bar and nothing leaves it.
